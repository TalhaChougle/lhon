ABOUT US

YOUNG HANDS. BIG HEARTS. REAL CHANGE.

Leo Hands of Nurture is a youth-led, volunteer-driven initiative operating under the Leo Club ecosystem — affiliated with Lions International and proudly rooted in Makassar, South Sulawesi, Indonesia.

We are young people who believe that compassion, action, and community can change lives. Our volunteers drive programs in English literacy, healthcare advocacy, and community support — giving our neighbors access to care and opportunity they deserve.

EDUCATION Ongoing Program

Opening Doors: English Literacy at the Orphanage

Every Saturday morning, a group of Leo Hands of Nurture volunteers arrives at a local orphanage in Makassar with lesson materials, games, and a lot of enthusiasm. For many of the children there, this is the only structured English learning they receive.

English opens doors. It builds confidence in students who want to compete in a wider world — whether in higher education, work, or simply communicating with people beyond their community. Our volunteers don't just teach language. They show up, week after week, as friends and mentors.

If you want to join us as a teaching volunteer, or if your school or organization wants to collaborate on expanding the program, we'd love to hear from you.

COMMUNITY Ongoing Program

More Than Donations: Being Present

It is easy to donate goods. It is harder — and more meaningful — to show up. Leo Hands of Nurture doesn't treat community support as a transaction. We visit. We sit down. We listen. We share meals, play games, and spend real time with the children and families we serve.

Our orphanage support program includes regular visits, seasonal donation drives, and holiday programs — but the heart of it is human connection. The children we visit don't just need supplies. They need to know people care about them.

If you want to be part of this program as a volunteer, or if you'd like to contribute to our donation drives, reach out through our contact form below.
